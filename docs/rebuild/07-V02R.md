$$\boxed{\text{Given that we know what happened, \emph{what should be changed?}}}$$

**This version has no online learning loop.** It compares *representations of
where change should occur*, scored directly against the evaluator's repair truth.

## 1. Why there is no learning loop here

The natural design would be: fix some update primitive, vary the credit unit,
and pick the unit with the better learning curve. That design is rejected, and
the reason is the central methodological correction of the rebuild.

If the fixed primitive — say `NegativeOnly` — is itself poor, then

$$R_A + \texttt{NegativeOnly} \;\;>\;\; R_B + \texttt{NegativeOnly}$$

does **not** establish that $R_A$ is the better representation. The winner may be
decided entirely by a $Unit \times Primitive$ interaction. The legacy project made
exactly this mistake in a different form: it compared arms whose differences were
produced by a hidden assumption rather than by the factor under study.

$$\boxed{\text{Do not select a representation with an unvalidated primitive.}}$$

So V0.2R removes the primitive from the loop entirely and asks a purely
representational question, answerable without any training:

> Which credit representation most faithfully expresses *where the change should
> occur*?

**V0.2R selects no representation and crowns no winner.** Its four arms are scored
side by side against a fixed truth, and the result is a set of *failure-mode
profiles*, not a ranking to be inherited. The primitive's turn comes in V0.3R, whose
entry condition is therefore **not** "inherit the V0.2R winner" but

$$\boxed{\text{V0.3R conditions on the A65/A67 working ontology } \Gamma}$$

That is the only thing V0.2R can hand over: a fixed unit ontology together with a
measured account of what each compression of it costs. Whether $\Gamma$ is the
*right* ontology is not something V0.2R can decide (§8).

## 2. Truth being predicted

**Rewritten by A65/A67.** The old text made one object — "the minimal sufficient
intervention set" — do the work of two, and treated a *rescue* as *credit truth*.
A57 shows why that fails: $do(C_P = \text{identity})$ repairs the process-commit
mechanism, while $do(z = z')$ may merely route around it by running a different
strategy. So the identity

$$\text{minimal sufficient rescue} \Rightarrow \text{credit truth}$$

is withdrawn, and V0.2R predicts the **credit-unit set**

$$\Gamma^\ast(\ell) = \{\,\pi_{\text{credit}}(r) : r \in \mathcal R^{\text{mech},\ast}(\ell)\,\}$$

projected from the **mechanism** repair, never from the rescue (`16-V02R-SEMANTICS.md`
§6). Two repair truths exist and both are minimal:

$$\mathcal R^{\text{mech},\ast}, \qquad \mathcal R^{\text{rescue},\ast}, \qquad
|R^\ast| \neq \#R^\ast \text{ in both}$$

$R^{\text{rescue}}$ is an evaluator diagnostic and **never mints credit truth**:
it can never change $\Gamma^\ast$, whether or not a strategy replay succeeds.

V0.1R predicted $C^{\text{fire}}$ (what is broken). V0.2R predicts $\Gamma^\ast$
(where responsibility for the faulty mechanism lies). Those are different objects,
and the distinction is what keeps the two versions separable.

**A71 corrected how $\Gamma^\ast$ is built.** It must come from $R^{\text{mech}}$
**descriptors**, not from the five fire bits: the bits say *which kind* fired, not
*which timestep or site*, so

$$Z^{\text{fire}}_{\text{truth}} \;\not\Rightarrow\; \Gamma^\ast_{\text{indexed}}$$

A truth built from generic type names intersected concretely-expanded predictions as
the empty set, which silently voided the first version of this census.

## 3. The four arms

Each arm is a **schema**: a rule that maps an input to a set of credit units.

| arm | input | output | role |
|---|---|---|---|
| `OracleCredit` | truth directly, **not** $X^{\text{obs}}$ | $\Gamma^\ast$ | evaluator-only **constructional ceiling** |
| `CausalSetLocator` | $X^{\text{loc}} = (\text{rows}, Z^{\text{fire}})$ | $\Gamma^+(X^{\text{loc}})$ | **known-SCM, information-limited exact set locator** |
| `Module` | $X^{\text{obs}}$ | $\eta_{\text{module}} \circ R_{\text{module}}(X^{\text{obs}})$ | coarse: H/L |
| `Trajectory` | $X^{\text{obs}}$ | $\eta_{\text{trajectory}} \circ R_{\text{trajectory}}(X^{\text{obs}})$ | coarsest: the whole episode |

`OracleCredit` is $R_{\text{repair}}$ renamed: it receives the truth and is not a
competitor. No method may consult the evaluator's enumeration, so its row **bounds**
what any representation could achieve rather than reporting what a method does.

**`R_causal` is gone, and that is A71's result, not a simplification.** The old fine
arm emitted $\pi_{\text{credit}}(\text{descriptor}_i)$ for each fired cause, which
collapsed to $\pi_{\text{credit}} \circ Z^{\text{fire}}_{\text{truth}}$ and was
therefore *identical to the ceiling* — and, worse, **ill-defined**: with $Z^{\text{fire}}$
alone there is no way to choose which `ControllerSite` address a fired $Z_X$ refers
to. A71 measured that ambiguity (14 of 893 locator classes, 0.1881% of DGP mass), so
the fine arm is now the **set-valued** `CausalSetLocator`, built from the public SCM
by factual-consistency inversion and gated at 893/893 against an independently
sourced evaluator reference. Its output is the envelope $\Gamma^+$, with $\Gamma^-$
as the diagnostic.

**The units and their writability** are frozen by A65/A67/A69:

```text
credit_unit      agent_writeable      credit_unit       agent_writeable
ProcessCommit    true                 ExternalPlant     false
Decision_t       true                 Unknown/NoWrite   false
ControllerSite   true                 Strategy          false
```

`agent_writeable` is defined in `17-V02R-METHOD-CONTRACT.md` §6 as "V0.3R's frozen
update primitive may write persistently to this unit". `Strategy`'s value is
**assigned there**, not inferred: being able to run a runtime intervention
$do(z = z')$ is not the same as a version being permitted a persistent write, so
its flag is not derivable from the existence of that intervention. A67 left it
unassigned; A69 fixes it at `false` and any version that later makes strategy a
write target must amend that explicitly.

`Strategy` is a legitimate **rescue atom** and appears in no mechanism-credit
truth — verified on all 1,038,960 worlds, where `Strategy` occurs in
$\Gamma^\ast$ zero times. That is not a gap in the ontology; it is the distinction
V0.2R exists to measure.

**H/L is not banned, but nothing is adopted or removed here.** A70–A74 replaced the
adopt-or-remove decision with an exact census: `Module` is scored, its cost is
measured, and the number stands on its own. A later version that wants to drop or
adopt a granularity must say so explicitly; V0.2R neither.

## 4. Endpoints

Each arm induces a set of proposed units $\hat\Gamma$, computed against $\Gamma^\ast$
and **never** against another arm's output.

| endpoint | definition | role |
|---|---|---|
| **Coverage** | $\lvert\hat\Gamma \cap \Gamma^\ast\rvert / \lvert\Gamma^\ast\rvert$ | **co-primary** |
| **FalseCreditRate** | $\lvert\hat\Gamma \setminus \Gamma^\ast\rvert / \max(1, \lvert\hat\Gamma\rvert)$ | **co-primary** |
| OutcomeRepairSufficiency | fraction of episodes where applying $\hat\Gamma$ (via a fixed canonical conversion rule) actually makes the episode succeed | secondary / evaluator diagnostic |
| $R^{*}$-Coverage, SitePrecision, SiteRecall | as before, now computed on $\Gamma^\ast$ | secondary |
| CandidateSetSize | mean $\#\hat\Gamma$ | secondary — an arm that proposes everything scores well on recall and is useless |
| Tie handling | does the arm admit multiple candidates when $\#R^{*} > 1$? | **structural, pass/fail** |

### 4.0 Why `InterventionSufficiency` is no longer primary

It requires a `credit unit → canonical intervention` conversion rule, and that
conversion is already most of V0.3R's repair primitive. Measuring a
*representation* partly through a *repair rule* reintroduces exactly the
contamination A65 removes. Coverage and FCR ask the narrower and prior question:
does the representation express **the right place to change at all**.

### 4.1 Why coverage and FCR are co-primary

Coverage alone rewards proposing everything; FCR alone rewards proposing nothing.
Together they resist both — and **A74 showed they do not degenerate**: the two are
needed at *different* points of the same population (§4.3).

`ExternalPlant` stays **in the denominator** even though no agent can write it: it
is a definite credit *location*, and deciding not to act on
`agent_writeable = false` is V0.3R's business, not V0.2R's. So `credit location`
$\neq$ `agent writeability`.

| truth | prediction | Coverage | FCR |
|---|---|---|---|
| $\{\texttt{ExternalPlant}\}$ | $\{\texttt{ExternalPlant}\}$ | 1 | 0 |
| $\{\texttt{ExternalPlant}\}$ | $\{\texttt{Strategy}\}$ — **even when the strategy rescue succeeded** | 0 | 1 |
| $\{\texttt{ExternalPlant}\}$ | $\{\texttt{ExternalPlant}, \texttt{Strategy}\}$ | 1 | 1/2 |

The full census found **33,960** worlds in which a strategy replay rescues the
outcome while the frozen truth remains `ExternalPlant`, plus 1,080 where no
mechanism was repaired at all and a strategy replay still rescued. Those are the
discrimination witnesses that make the middle row non-vacuous.

**Abstention is not a verdict.** $\varnothing \neq \{\texttt{Unknown/NoWrite}\}$, and
$\text{Coverage}(\varnothing,\Gamma^\ast) = \text{FCR}(\varnothing,\Gamma^\ast) = 0$
(A69 §5). A prediction of $\varnothing$ means "propose nothing"; `Unknown/NoWrite`
means "the ontology contains no writable mechanism to blame". They must never be
scored alike, which is the rule that catches `Module` below.

### 4.2 The canonical conversion rule

Retained, but **only for the secondary endpoint**. Converting a credit unit into a
canonical intervention is a repair-side operation, so it must not enter the primary
comparison. The rule is still required to be identical across arms and
declared in the preregistration.

### 4.3 Primary result — the exact full-support census

Primary is an **exact enumeration over all 1,038,960 worlds** of the frozen support,
not a sampled experiment: every arm is a deterministic rule and the population is
DGP-enumerable, so drawing scenes would add no statistical content (A70, retained).
Both a world-count mean and a DGP-weighted mean are reported, because they differ by
an order of magnitude on this support.

| arm | Coverage (count) | Coverage (mass) | FCR (count) | FCR (mass) | $\lvert\hat\Gamma\rvert$ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `OracleCredit` | 1.0000 | 1.0000 | 0.0000 | 0.0000 | 1.71 |
| `CausalSetLocator` | 1.0000 | 1.0000 | 0.0028 | **0.0006** | 1.72 |
| `Module` | 0.9834 | **0.5614** | 0.7958 | **0.4622** | 12.81 |
| `Trajectory` | 1.0000 | 1.0000 | 0.9017 | **0.9309** | 17.90 |

`OracleCredit`'s perfect row is a **constructional identity**, asserted rather than
reported as a finding.

The informative content is not "who wins" but **three different failure modes**:

$$\text{CausalSetLocator}: \text{a very thin unidentifiability} \Rightarrow
\text{a very small over-credit}$$

$$\text{Module}: \text{abstains when nothing fired} \;+\; \text{coarse over-credit when
something did}$$

$$\text{Trajectory}: \text{always covers the truth, but blames almost the entire
episode}$$

`CausalSetLocator` over-credits only the 0.1881% of DGP mass where the evidence is
genuinely ambiguous (A71). `Trajectory` covers everything and is therefore useless
as a location. `Module` sits between and is the only arm whose Coverage drops at all
— which is precisely why the pair is co-primary.

**`Module`'s missing Coverage is exactly its abstention region**, and this is
machine-checked: $Z^{\text{fire}} = 00000$ covers **17,280 worlds = 43.86% of DGP
mass**, and

$$1 - 0.438594 = 0.561406 = \text{Coverage}_{\text{Module}}(\text{mass})$$

with residual $2.8\times10^{-12}$ (summation order). Coverage punishes abstention;
FCR punishes over-credit. Neither alone is sufficient.

**Claim boundary, and it is binding.** $\Gamma^+$ is built from the *same*
$\pi_{\text{credit}}$ as the truth. This census therefore measures over-credit under
a **fixed** ontology. It does **not** validate that ontology — see §8.

## 5. The secondary probe, and its hard restriction

Every arm additionally undergoes the *same* blunt probe: apply
$-\delta$ to the units it proposes, and measure damage.

$$\text{WithinUnitDamage}, \quad \text{CrossUnitCollateral}, \quad \text{EditCount}$$

**Restriction:**

$$\boxed{\text{The probe's task-utility AUC may \textbf{not} be used to select a representation for V0.3R.}}$$

The probe is reported because it is cheap and diagnostic. It is not allowed to
decide, because a blunt constant edit interacts with the representation for
reasons that have nothing to do with whether the representation is faithful.

## 6. The interaction guard (end of V0.3R, not here)

After V0.3R fixes its primitive, a small bridge experiment runs

$$2\ \text{granularities} \times 2\ \text{primitives}$$

for one purpose only: to confirm that the granularity ordering measured in §4.3 has
not been completely overturned by a granularity × primitive interaction. It is **not**
a re-selection of a representation, and it carries no primary hypothesis. There is no
"V0.2R winner" to inherit.

If the bridge finds a strong interaction, that is a finding in its own right — it
would mean credit granularities cannot be ranked independently of update rules,
which would be an important negative result about the whole research programme.

## 7. Not in this version

* no policy learning, no Q updates, no learning curves;
* no learned attribution — V0.2R receives **cause truth** $Z^{\text{fire}}$, because
  V0.1R already owns the inference question and mixing them would re-entangle the
  chain;
* no candidate scoring $\hat V(c) - \lambda U(c) - \beta C_{\text{switch}}(c)$ —
  that is V0.4R.

### 8.1 The "failure ⇒ the ontology is wrong" inference is WITHDRAWN

An earlier version of this section said that if V0.2R failed, *the notion of a credit
unit this project is using is wrong*, and the programme should stop and rethink the
representation. **That inference cannot be drawn**, and the reason is structural:

$$\boxed{\text{V0.2R's scoring truth is itself built on the working ontology } \Gamma}$$

$\Gamma^\ast$ is produced by $\pi_{\text{credit}}$ over the frozen unit set, and
$\Gamma^+$ by the same mapping. An experiment cannot use its own definition of the
truth to adjudicate whether that definition is *correct*; it can only measure the
consequences of **compressing** it. So the correct reading of a V0.2R failure is:

* the coarse representations lose information under $\Gamma$ (measured in §4.3), and
* localisation under a known SCM is limited by a thin unidentifiability (A71),

and **not** that $\Gamma$ is wrong. Adjudicating $\Gamma$ needs an **external
criterion** that is not itself defined by $\Gamma^\ast$ — a downstream consequence.
That is V0.3R's problem, and it is why V0.3R's entry condition is stated in §1 as
"conditions on the working ontology $\Gamma$" rather than "inherits the winner".
