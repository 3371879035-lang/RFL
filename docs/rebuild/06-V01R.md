# 06 — V0.1R: Observation/Evidence → Cause inference

$$\boxed{\text{Can the system work out \emph{what happened}?}}$$

**No reinforcement learning happens in this version.** There is no policy update,
no Q-table, no credit assignment, no learning loop of any kind.

This is the point. If V0.1R fails, the conclusion is exactly one sentence —
*the system cannot interpret its own feedback* — with no possibility of blaming
the update rule, the credit unit, the training budget, or the environment's
difficulty. The legacy project entangled all of those in a single number.

---

## 1. Primary hypothesis

> Given unreliable diagnostic feedback and the agent's own sensory/command
> evidence, sequence evidence plus controlled queries (interventions and audits)
> recovers the **fired-mechanism label** $Z^{\text{fire}}$ more accurately than
> taking feedback at face value.

$$H_{0.1R}:\quad \text{AUPRC}_{\text{Seq}\to\text{Query}} > \text{AUPRC}_{\text{DirectFeedback}} + \Delta_{\min}$$

---

## 2. What is predicted

The **fired-mechanism vector** $Z^{\text{fire}} = (Z^{\text{fire}}_P, \ldots, Z^{\text{fire}}_U) \in \{0,1\}^5$, multi-label (`02-SCM.md` §1, `11` §6.1), after the identifiability merge (`03-IDENTIFIABILITY.md` §4).

**Not $Z^{\text{pres}}$ and not $B$** (**A54**). A fault that was configured but
never executed on the trajectory did not *happen*, and this version asks what
happened; predicting $B$ would swap diagnosis for causal contribution. The two
are reported separately as diagnostics and neither licenses this version. As
always $Z^{\text{pres}} \ge Z^{\text{fire}}$, so a method that predicts absence
everywhere is wrong on every fired mechanism and the metrics see it.

The method emits $p = (p_P, p_D, p_X, p_E, p_U)$, **not** constrained to sum to 1.
If a method normalises internally it must also report the unnormalised scores it
would have produced.

---

## 3. Arms

**A56 renamed the budget-bearing arms.** Gate_fire's PASS depends on the A53
plant audit and the A55 process-proposal audit, so an arm restricted to
counterfactual rollouts would have a strictly *narrower* information set than the
gate credited — the gate would be certifying a method that cannot exist. The arms
therefore spend a single shared query budget over **interventions and audits
together**.

| arm | evidence used | queries | role |
|---|---|---|---|
| `DirectFeedback` | feedback channel only | 0 | the straw man: take feedback at face value |
| `SequenceEvidence` | $obs_{1..T}$ | 0 | evidence without intervention |
| `QueryOnly` | interventions + audits, no sequence evidence | $B_Q$ | querying without evidence |
| **`SeqThenQuery`** | sequence evidence, then targeted interventions + audits | $B_Q$ | **the hypothesis** |
| `Oracle` | evaluator truth | — | ceiling / sanity |

`SeqThenQuery` must spend its budget *informed by* the sequence evidence;
`QueryOnly` spends the same budget blindly. Equal $B_Q$ across all budget-bearing
arms is mandatory — otherwise the comparison measures budget, not method. The
rename from `CFOnly` / `SeqThenCF` is **semantic, not cosmetic**: the old names
asserted that only counterfactual rollouts count as queries, which stopped being
true at A53 and A55.

An audit costs 1, like an intervention (`03` §3.1), and the two kinds compete for
the same $B_Q$. An arm is free to spend all of it on audits, or none.

### 3.1 The Oracle arm is a gate, not a result

`Oracle` fed the true $Z^{\text{fire}}$ must score at the metric's ceiling. If it
does not, the labels or the metrics are broken and **the version does not run**.
The legacy lesson: an "Oracle" arm can be mis-implemented and produce a false
negative that looks like a scientific finding.

The ceiling is taken **on $Z^{\text{fire}}$**, not on $Z^{\text{pres}}$ and not on
$B$: feeding the old target would certify a ceiling for a question this version
does not ask. $Z^{\text{pres}}$ and $B$ get their own oracle rows as diagnostics
only.

## 4. Endpoints

| endpoint | role |
|---|---|
| **macro AUPRC over causes** | **primary** |
| macro AUROC over causes | secondary |
| exact-set accuracy | secondary — will be harsh at 5 independent labels; reported, not primary |
| Hamming distance | secondary |
| Brier score | secondary |
| ECE (calibration) | secondary — required because $p$ is unnormalised |
| unknown-channel detection (AUPRC on $c_U$) | secondary, **but pre-registered** |
| counterfactual queries used | **always reported** |

$\Delta_{\min}$ is declared for the primary endpoint before collection.

### 4.1 Why AUPRC and not accuracy

Causes are sparse: most episodes have zero or one active cause. Accuracy is
dominated by the negative class and would let a method that predicts "nothing is
wrong" score well. AUPRC is the appropriate primary for rare positives, and the
per-cause breakdown is mandatory so that a method cannot win by being good at
only the common cause.

### 4.2 The unknown channel is a first-class endpoint

$c_U$ is the cause the hypothesis space cannot represent. A method that never
emits $p_U > 0$ is not merely inaccurate — it is **structurally unable** to report
"I do not know", and that must be visible in the results rather than hidden inside
an aggregate. Case **C5** in `04-SEMANTIC-INVARIANTS.md` fails such a method by
construction.

---

## 5. Query budget

$B_Q$ is frozen before collection and reported with every result. It counts
**all** queries — interventions and audits alike, each cost 1 — and its frozen
value is $B_Q = 4$. A sweep over $B_Q \in \{0, 1, 2, 5, 20\}$ is a **secondary**
contrast and is not the primary claim; the primary claim is stated at one frozen
$B_Q$.

Recorded so the pass is read with its true width: Gate_fire passes with
$B_{\min}^{\text{adaptive}} = 3$ against $B_Q = 4$, i.e. **one unit of slack**,
where every earlier gate had three or more. Adding an identifiability requirement
without re-running Gate_fire would break it.

---

## 6. Not in this version

* any Q update, any policy change, any learning curve;
* reward shaping (reward is a *secondary factorial* — see below);
* credit units, repair, update primitive — those are V0.2R and V0.3R.

### 6.1 Reward is demoted to a secondary factorial

$$\mathbb{E}[R^{(A)}] = 2P(S)-1, \qquad \mathbb{E}[R^{(B)}] = P(S) \qquad\text{— \textbf{withdrawn, see A14}}$$

That identity held only **without a policy-dependent step cost**. With the frozen
$-0.02$ per step and route lengths of 4 / 6 / 8, the returns are
$G_A = 2\cdot\mathbf{1}_{\text{success}} - 1 - 0.02L$ and
$G_B = \mathbf{1}_{\text{success}} - 0.02L$ with $L$ policy-dependent, so the
objectives are **not** affine transforms and do **not** in general share an optimal
policy. Reward × Update is therefore a genuine factorial between two different
objectives, not a null manipulation.

This does not block the Gate, which runs on the **primary reward mode A** only. It
must be cleaned before any tier-2 reward condition is run.

Here reward enters only as a crossed factor:
$\text{reward} \times \text{evidence}$, asking whether the *outcome channel*
interacts with inference. It is tier-2 and cannot support a primary claim.

## 7. Go / no-go

| gate | condition |
|---|---|
| **Identifiability Gate_fire** (`03` §1.4) | PASS — every **fired-mechanism** pattern $Z^{\text{fire}}$ separable within $B_Q$ |
| Identifiability Gate_Z (`03` §1.4) | *diagnostic only* — presence identifiability; expected to fail on dormant faults and **not** a blocker |
| Identifiability Gate_B (`03` §1.4) | *secondary, non-blocking* — if $B$ is not identifiable it is reported **not evaluable** (`11` §6.2), never as an algorithm negative result |
| Semantic suite (`04`) | PASS |
| Oracle ceiling | `Oracle` ≥ declared ceiling on the primary endpoint |
| Development calibration | benchmark has discriminative range at $N_{\text{scenes}}=32$ |

**V0.1R's dependent variable is $Z^{\text{fire}}$** (§2), not $Z^{\text{pres}}$
and not $B$. A configured fault that never executed on the trajectory did not
*happen*, and V0.1R asks what happened; predicting $B$ instead would swap
diagnosis for causal contribution. The change from $Z^{\text{pres}}$ to
$Z^{\text{fire}}$ is a **scientific-question revision** (**A54**), and it is not
a relaxation — $Z^{\text{fire}}$ is still a five-bit diagnosis target recovered
from evidence.

**Current status: Gate_fire PASSES.** Depth histogram $D = 0$ 1,553, $D = 1$ 990,
$D = 2$ 230, $D = 3$ 10, unidentifiable at any depth **0**, so
$B_{\min}^{\text{adaptive}} = 3 \le B_Q = 4$. The 600-class residual that A54
measured was 100% $P$ and is closed by A55's process proposal audit; $E$ was
closed by A53's plant audit, and $X$, $D$, $U$ by the A54 split.

**The margin is one unit**, and that is worth stating rather than smoothing over:
every earlier gate had three or more units of slack, so this is the first time
$B_Q$ is anywhere near binding and a single added identifiability requirement
would break it. $B_Q$ is not raised.

**V0.1R's identifiability precondition is met for the first time**, so seed
collection may resume — subject to the remaining rows below, which have not been
run. It is not started here.

The diagnostic gates remain FAILED and remain **non-blocking**: Gate_Z on 2,713
(dormant faults), Gate_B on 135 (counterfactual secondary, reported **not
evaluable** per `11` §6.2; note it needs depth 4 for 20 classes, so for that
endpoint the budget *is* binding). The identifiable quotient
$\widetilde{\mathcal B} = \mathcal B/{\sim}$ keeps $(D, X, U)$ and discards
$(P, E)$ — **A52**.

**If V0.1R fails:** the chain stops here. The finding is *the system cannot
interpret its own feedback*, and no amount of work on credit, repair, or update
rules is indicated. This is the first and cheapest place for the whole programme
to die, which is why it is first. It has now been made **runnable**, which is what
this whole gate sequence was for.
